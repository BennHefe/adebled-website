Programmes

Two practical tracks.

Both are taught by people who repair and support computers for a living, through our IT Services division.

Repair & maintenance

Computer Engineering

Learn to take computers apart, find the fault and put them back together working. Taught on the same kind of machines our repair bench sees every day.

  • Computer components and how they fit together
  • Desktop and laptop disassembly and reassembly
  • Fault finding and diagnostics
  • RAM, SSD and hard drive upgrades
  • Operating system installation and recovery
  • Preventive maintenance and cleaning

Troubleshooting & helpdesk

IT Support

Learn to keep people and offices working: set up machines, solve everyday problems and support users in person or remotely.

  • Windows setup, user accounts and drivers
  • Troubleshooting common hardware and software problems
  • Networking basics: Wi-Fi, routers and cabling
  • Printers and peripherals
  • Email, Microsoft Office and backups
  • Security basics and remote support tools
