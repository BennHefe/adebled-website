Questions

Good to know.

Is the site measurement really free?

Yes. We measure the openings at no cost, so what we fabricate fits.

Do you work outside Lagos?

Yes. The workshop is in Lagos, and we deliver and install across Nigeria.

Can you make something that is not on the list?

Yes. Send a drawing or a photo of what you have in mind and we will tell you what is possible.

What should I send when I enquire?

Your location, what you need and rough sizes if you have them. Photos of the openings help.
